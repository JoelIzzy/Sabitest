# Sabitest - Virtual Number & Social Media Boosting Platform

## What is Sabitest?

Sabitest is a comprehensive Nigerian digital services platform that enables businesses and individuals to purchase virtual phone numbers for OTP verification and boost their social media presence through automated services.

## Core Services

### 1. Virtual Numbers (SMS Verification)
- **Purpose:** Receive SMS messages for OTP verification without using personal phone numbers
- **Use Cases:**
  - Account verification on WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook, Instagram
  - Banking and fintech app verification
  - E-commerce platform registration
  - Business account creation
- **Supported Services:**
  - WhatsApp
  - Telegram
  - Facebook
  - Instagram
  - Google
  - TikTok
  - X (Twitter)
  - OPay, Kuda, Uber
- **Countries:** Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, South Africa, USA, UK, Canada, India

### 2. Social Media Boosting (SMM)
- **Purpose:** Increase social media engagement through automated services
- **Services Offered:**
  - Instagram Followers & Likes
  - TikTok Followers & Views
  - Facebook Page Likes
  - YouTube Views
  - X (Twitter) Followers
  - Telegram Channel Members
- **Features:**
  - Real followers (not bots)
  - High retention rates
  - Drip-feed options for natural growth
  - Refill support for lost followers
  - 30-day refill guarantee on selected services

## How It Works

### For Virtual Numbers:
1. **Select Service** - Choose the platform (WhatsApp, Telegram, etc.)
2. **Select Country** - Choose the country for the virtual number
3. **Purchase** - Pay using your wallet balance
4. **Receive Number** - Get a temporary phone number
5. **Get OTP** - Receive SMS with verification code
6. **Auto-Detection** - System automatically extracts OTP codes
7. **20-Minute Window** - Numbers are held for 20 minutes to receive SMS

### For Social Boosting:
1. **Select Service** - Choose the desired service (followers, likes, views)
2. **Enter Link** - Provide your social media post or profile link
3. **Set Quantity** - Specify the number of followers/likes/views needed
4. **Purchase** - Pay using wallet balance
5. **Automated Delivery** - Service provider delivers the boost
6. **Track Progress** - Monitor delivery status in real-time
7. **Refund Protection** - Automatic refunds for undelivered orders
